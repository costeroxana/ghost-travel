## Prompts

App icon: A polished, friendly white ghost with navy eyes, a flowing flight-trail body and a mint paper airplane pointing upper right. Bold silhouette, generous safe margins, indigo #4f46e5 to navy #13274a full-bleed background, mint #85dfce accents. Contemporary flat illustrative logo, subtle depth. No text, frame, mockup or watermark.

Android foreground reference edit: Preserve the approved ghost and paper airplane, remove the background, center the mark with generous padding on a transparent square.

Social preview reference edit: Match the approved icon and palette on a wide 2:1 banner, ghost at left, title "Ghost Travel", subtitle "Your next adventure, thoughtfully planned.", and "Android · iOS · Web". Subtle flight paths and clear safe margins.

New native builds are required to apply launcher icons; Expo Go keeps its own launcher icon.
